### Agenda
- **Share your proposals.** Everyone shows their idea, and we give each other feedback. As you listen, think about:
  - How does someone interact with it?
  - What does it sense, and how does it respond: with light, sound or movement?
  - What's the hardest part to build, and what's a simple way to test it first?
- **DC motors.** A quick demo. The vibration motor on your Drawing Machine is a tiny DC motor with an off-balance weight on it. A bigger DC motor needs more power than the Arduino can give, so I'll connect one to the Arduino Nano 33 IoT through an Adafruit DRV8871 motor driver. The driver switches a separate power supply on and off for the motor, so the Arduino can set its speed with `analogWrite()` and reverse its direction. If your project needs wheels, a fan or anything else that keeps turning, this is how.
- **What's next.** We'll go over the rest of the semester on the [Final Project](./content/Assignments/Final_Project/final_project.html) page. Next week you'll start testing your idea with a rough physical version.
