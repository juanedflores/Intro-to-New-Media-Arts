Setting up the Class Band

Every student uploads the same class_button sketch. Each board sends its button over Wi-Fi to this laptop, and each board gets its own sound and animation, in the order the boards connect.

In class

  1. Plug in the router and join this laptop to its network.
  2. Double-click start_class_band.command in the station folder. This page opens. Click it once to turn on the sound.
  3. Students wire a button to pin 2 (3.3V → button → pin 2, 10kΩ from pin 2 to GND), type the Wi-Fi name and password into the sketch, and upload.

Players

  • Each board gets a chip at the bottom with its name and its sound. Names come from the Class Sensor Grid (same browser), or the board ID.
  • Click a chip to give that board the next sound. Shift-click to rename it.
  • Boards running the Velostat class_sensor sketch work too: a hard squeeze is a press.

Keys

A–Z play the sounds · Space new colors · Shift+H hide the chips and buttons · Shift+F full screen · ? this help
