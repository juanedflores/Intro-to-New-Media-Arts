# Resources

<p class="rs-intro">Places to look things up, learn a skill, find parts, and keep
researching after class.</p>

::: {#rs-research .rs-section short="Research"}
## Research &amp; Inspiration

- [Are.na: ART 150 Fall 2026](https://www.are.na/art-150-fall-2026) Our class group: everyone's channels and research in one place.
- [Rhizome](https://rhizome.org/) Born-digital art and culture, with the ArtBase archive of net art.
- [Creative Applications Network](https://www.creativeapplications.net/) Projects at the edge of art, design and technology, with notes on how they were made.
- [Ars Electronica Archive](https://ars.electronica.art/archive/en/) Decades of Prix Ars Electronica winners and festival projects.
- [Media Art Net](http://www.medienkunstnetz.de/) Survey of media art history: artists, works and themes.
- [Monoskop](https://monoskop.org/) Wiki and library on media art, culture and theory.
- [UbuWeb](https://www.ubu.com/) Avant-garde film, sound and writing, free to view.
- [Google Arts &amp; Culture](https://artsandculture.google.com/) Museum collections and online exhibitions from around the world.
- [The Wrong Biennale](https://thewrong.org/) A huge, global, online-first biennale of digital art.
- [Smithsonian Online Exhibitions](https://www.si.edu/exhibitions/online) Virtual exhibitions from the Smithsonian museums.
:::

::: {#rs-library .rs-section short="Libraries"}
## Libraries &amp; Papers

- [UIC Library](https://library.uic.edu/) Books, databases and research help. Sign in with your UIC NetID.
- [ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org/) Papers on computing, interaction design and media (SIGGRAPH, CHI, NIME).
- [Internet Archive](https://archive.org/) Free books, software, films, audio and old websites (the Wayback Machine).
- [Leonardo (MIT Press)](https://direct.mit.edu/leon) The journal of art, science and technology.
:::

::: {#rs-electronics .rs-section short="Electronics"}
## Electronics &amp; Arduino

- [Makeability Lab: Physical Computing](https://makeabilitylab.github.io/physcomp/) The free textbook we use in class: electronics, Arduino and sensors.
- [Adafruit Learning System](https://learn.adafruit.com/) Step-by-step guides for nearly every part Adafruit sells.
- [SparkFun Tutorials](https://learn.sparkfun.com/tutorials) Clear explanations of the basics: voltage, resistors, soldering and more.
- [Arduino Project Hub](https://projecthub.arduino.cc/) Projects shared by the Arduino community, with code and wiring.
- [Arduino Language Reference](https://docs.arduino.cc/language-reference/) Every Arduino function, with examples.
- [Instructables](https://www.instructables.com/) How-tos for making almost anything.
- [Hackaday](https://hackaday.com/) Daily hardware hacks and projects.
- [Make:](https://makezine.com/) Projects, skill builders and maker news.
- [Tinkercad Circuits](https://www.tinkercad.com/learn/circuits) Build and simulate Arduino circuits in the browser.
- [Fritzing](https://fritzing.org/) Draw breadboard diagrams of your circuits.
- [Circuit Symbols](https://electronicsclub.info/circuitsymbols.htm) A guide to reading schematics.
- [Electronics Tutorials](https://www.electronics-tutorials.ws/) In-depth explanations of components and circuits.
:::

::: {#rs-code .rs-section short="Coding"}
## Creative Coding &amp; Software

- [p5.js](https://p5js.org/) Creative coding in the browser, with a friendly editor and lots of examples.
- [Processing](https://processing.org/) The sketchbook-style coding environment p5.js grew out of.
- [The Coding Train](https://thecodingtrain.com/) Daniel Shiffman's video lessons on p5.js, Processing and Arduino.
- [The Nature of Code](https://natureofcode.com/) Shiffman's free book on simulating natural systems in code.
- [Hydra](https://hydra.ojack.xyz/) Live-code video synthesis in the browser.
- [Pure Data](https://puredata.info/) Free visual programming for sound and media.
- [Max tutorials (Cycling '74)](https://cycling74.com/tutorials) Getting started with Max/MSP.
- [openFrameworks](https://openframeworks.cc/) C++ toolkit for creative coding.
- [Blender](https://www.blender.org/) Free 3D modeling, animation and rendering.
- [A-Frame](https://aframe.io/) Build VR and AR scenes for the web.
- [OBS Studio](https://obsproject.com/) Free screen recording and live streaming. Handy for documentation.
- [Unity](https://unity.com/) Game engine for interactive 3D and VR.
- [Raspberry Pi](https://www.raspberrypi.com/) Small, cheap computers for installations.
:::

::: {#rs-sound .rs-section short="Sound"}
## Sound

- [Ableton: Learning Synths](https://learningsynths.ableton.com/) Learn synthesis by playing with one in the browser.
- [Ableton: Learning Music](https://learningmusic.ableton.com/) Beats, melody and song structure, hands-on.
- [Ishkur's Guide to Electronic Music](https://music.ishkur.com/) An interactive map of electronic music genres.
- [Freesound](https://freesound.org/) Free, Creative Commons sound effects and recordings.
- [NASA Audio Collection](https://archive.org/details/nasaaudiocollection) Space mission recordings at the Internet Archive.
- [Experimental Sound Studio](https://ess.org/) Chicago's home for sound art: performances, residencies and an archive.
:::

::: {#rs-making .rs-section short="Making"}
## Making &amp; Materials

- [Smooth-On: Basics of Mold Making](https://www.smooth-on.com/howto/basics-mold-making/) Molding and casting, from the people who make the rubber.
- [Acrylic Cheat Sheet](https://makezine.com/article/workshop/skill-builder-acrylic/) How to cut, glue and bend acrylic.
- [Post-Processing 3D Prints](https://www.instructables.com/Post-Processing-3D-Printed-Objects/) Sanding, filling and painting printed parts.
- [Paper Mache Recipes](https://www.ultimatepapermache.com/paper-mache-recipes) Five recipes for different kinds of sculpture.
- [Thingiverse](https://www.thingiverse.com/) Free 3D-printable models.
- [Sketchfab](https://sketchfab.com/) 3D models to view, download and use.
- [Smithsonian 3D](https://3d.si.edu/) 3D scans from the Smithsonian collections.
:::

::: {#rs-buy .rs-section short="Parts"}
## Where to Buy Parts

### Electronics online

- [Adafruit](https://www.adafruit.com/) Beginner-friendly parts with great guides (including Velostat).
- [SparkFun](https://www.sparkfun.com/) Sensors, boards and kits, each with a tutorial.
- [DigiKey](https://www.digikey.com/) Huge catalog of components. Ships fast.
- [Mouser](https://www.mouser.com/) Another huge component catalog.
- [Jameco](https://www.jameco.com/) Components, kits and tools.
- [Pololu](https://www.pololu.com/) Motors, motor drivers and robotics parts.
- [Tindie](https://www.tindie.com/) Small-batch hardware from independent makers.

### In Chicago

- [Micro Center](https://www.microcenter.com/) Arduinos, sensors and computer parts. Store on Elston Ave.
- [Creative Chicago Reuse Exchange](https://www.creativechirx.org/) Donated art and craft materials at low cost.
- [Rebuilding Exchange](https://www.rebuildingexchange.org/) Salvaged building materials.
- [Blick Art Materials](https://www.dickblick.com/) Art supplies, with several Chicago stores.

### Materials and fabrication

- [McMaster-Carr](https://www.mcmaster.com/) Every kind of hardware and raw material. Ships quickly from the suburbs.
- [TAP Plastics](https://www.tapplastics.com/) Acrylic, resin and plastics, cut to size.
- [KJ Magnetics](https://www.kjmagnetics.com/) Strong neodymium magnets.
- [OSH Park](https://oshpark.com/) Small, affordable runs of custom circuit boards.
- [PCBWay](https://www.pcbway.com/) Custom circuit boards and assembly.
:::
