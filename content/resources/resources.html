Resources

Places to look things up, learn a skill, find parts, and keep researching after class. Many of these come from the resource lists kept by SAIC’s Art & Technology Studies department.

Research & Inspiration

  • Are.na: ART 150 Fall 2026 Our class group: everyone’s channels and research in one place.
  • Rhizome Born-digital art and culture, with the ArtBase archive of net art.
  • Creative Applications Network Projects at the edge of art, design and technology, with notes on how they were made.
  • Ars Electronica Archive Decades of Prix Ars Electronica winners and festival projects.
  • Media Art Net Survey of media art history: artists, works and themes.
  • Monoskop Wiki and library on media art, culture and theory.
  • UbuWeb Avant-garde film, sound and writing, free to view.
  • Google Arts & Culture Museum collections and online exhibitions from around the world.
  • The Wrong Biennale A huge, global, online-first biennale of digital art.
  • Smithsonian Online Exhibitions Virtual exhibitions from the Smithsonian museums.

Libraries & Papers

  • UIC Library Books, databases and research help. Sign in with your UIC NetID.
  • ACM Digital Library Papers on computing, interaction design and media (SIGGRAPH, CHI, NIME).
  • Internet Archive Free books, software, films, audio and old websites (the Wayback Machine).
  • Leonardo (MIT Press) The journal of art, science and technology.

Electronics & Arduino

  • Makeability Lab: Physical Computing The free textbook we use in class: electronics, Arduino and sensors.
  • Adafruit Learning System Step-by-step guides for nearly every part Adafruit sells.
  • SparkFun Tutorials Clear explanations of the basics: voltage, resistors, soldering and more.
  • Arduino Project Hub Projects shared by the Arduino community, with code and wiring.
  • Arduino Language Reference Every Arduino function, with examples.
  • Instructables How-tos for making almost anything.
  • Hackaday Daily hardware hacks and projects.
  • Make: Projects, skill builders and maker news.
  • Tinkercad Circuits Build and simulate Arduino circuits in the browser.
  • Fritzing Draw breadboard diagrams of your circuits.
  • Circuit Symbols A guide to reading schematics.
  • Electronics Tutorials In-depth explanations of components and circuits.

Creative Coding & Software

  • p5.js Creative coding in the browser, with a friendly editor and lots of examples.
  • Processing The sketchbook-style coding environment p5.js grew out of.
  • The Coding Train Daniel Shiffman’s video lessons on p5.js, Processing and Arduino.
  • The Nature of Code Shiffman’s free book on simulating natural systems in code.
  • Hydra Live-code video synthesis in the browser.
  • Pure Data Free visual programming for sound and media.
  • Max tutorials (Cycling ’74) Getting started with Max/MSP.
  • openFrameworks C++ toolkit for creative coding.
  • Blender Free 3D modeling, animation and rendering.
  • A-Frame Build VR and AR scenes for the web.
  • OBS Studio Free screen recording and live streaming. Handy for documentation.
  • Unity Game engine for interactive 3D and VR.
  • Raspberry Pi Small, cheap computers for installations.

Sound

  • Ableton: Learning Synths Learn synthesis by playing with one in the browser.
  • Ableton: Learning Music Beats, melody and song structure, hands-on.
  • Ishkur’s Guide to Electronic Music An interactive map of electronic music genres.
  • Freesound Free, Creative Commons sound effects and recordings.
  • NASA Audio Collection Space mission recordings at the Internet Archive.
  • Experimental Sound Studio Chicago’s home for sound art: performances, residencies and an archive.

Making & Materials

  • Smooth-On: Basics of Mold Making Molding and casting, from the people who make the rubber.
  • Acrylic Cheat Sheet How to cut, glue and bend acrylic.
  • Post-Processing 3D Prints Sanding, filling and painting printed parts.
  • Paper Mache Recipes Five recipes for different kinds of sculpture.
  • Thingiverse Free 3D-printable models.
  • Sketchfab 3D models to view, download and use.
  • Smithsonian 3D 3D scans from the Smithsonian collections.

Where to Buy Parts

Electronics online

  • Adafruit Beginner-friendly parts with great guides (including Velostat).
  • SparkFun Sensors, boards and kits, each with a tutorial.
  • DigiKey Huge catalog of components. Ships fast.
  • Mouser Another huge component catalog.
  • Jameco Components, kits and tools.
  • Pololu Motors, motor drivers and robotics parts.
  • Tindie Small-batch hardware from independent makers.

In Chicago

  • Micro Center Arduinos, sensors and computer parts. Store on Elston Ave.
  • Creative Chicago Reuse Exchange Donated art and craft materials at low cost.
  • Rebuilding Exchange Salvaged building materials.
  • Blick Art Materials Art supplies, with several Chicago stores.

Materials and fabrication

  • McMaster-Carr Every kind of hardware and raw material. Ships quickly from the suburbs.
  • TAP Plastics Acrylic, resin and plastics, cut to size.
  • KJ Magnetics Strong neodymium magnets.
  • OSH Park Small, affordable runs of custom circuit boards.
  • PCBWay Custom circuit boards and assembly.
