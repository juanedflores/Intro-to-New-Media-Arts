## 1. The Physical & Performance-Based Line
### Richard Long: *A Line Made by Walking* (1967)

![A Line Made by Walking](https://i.imgur.com/XKUYPEP.png)

*   **Medium**: Land Art / Photography
*   **Concept**: A straight line created in a field by walking back and forth.
*   **Significance**: A record of physical movement, time, and the human trace on the landscape.

## 1. The Physical & Performance-Based Line
### Francis Alÿs: *The Green Line* (2004)

![The Green Line](https://i.imgur.com/ro0yIqh.png)

*   **Medium**: Performance / Intervention
*   **Concept**: Walking through Jerusalem with a leaking can of green paint.
*   **Significance**: Physically retracing a political armistice border to question territorial boundaries.

## 1. The Physical & Performance-Based Line
### Atsuko Tanaka: *Work (Bell)* (1955)

![Work Bell](https://i.imgur.com/YEMBJTR.png)

*   **Medium**: Sound Installation
*   **Concept**: A sequence of 20 alarm bells ringing in succession.
*   **Significance**: Described as a painting "in which the line is drawn clearly with one’s inner vision."

## 2. The Algorithmic & Robotic Line
### So Kanno & yang02: *Senseless Drawing Bot* (2011)

![Senseless Drawing Bot](https://publicdelivery.org/wp-content/uploads/2011/11/Takahiro-Yamaguchi-and-So-Kanno-%E2%80%93-Senseless-Drawing-Bot-1-800x534@2x.jpg)

*   **Medium**: Robotics
*   **Concept**: A self-propelling skateboard device that sprays abstract linework.
*   **Significance**: Explores the dynamism of graffiti and machine autonomy through a double pendulum.

## 2. The Algorithmic & Robotic Line
### A. Michael Noll: *Gaussian-Quadratic* (1963)

![Gaussian-Quadratic](https://i.imgur.com/kxXvMjq.png)

*   **Medium**: Computer-Generated Art
*   **Concept**: Early digital artwork consisting of lines generated through mathematical probability.
*   **Significance**: A foundational shift from the human hand to algorithmic logic.

---

## 2. The Algorithmic & Robotic Line
### Frieder Nake: *Matrix Multiplications* (1967)

![Matrix Multiplications](https://i.imgur.com/KRpELuj.png)

*   **Medium**: Generative Art
*   **Concept**: Uses mathematical matrices to determine the structure of lines.
*   **Significance**: Highlights the aesthetic potential of computational and systematic drawing.

## 3. Conceptual & Poetic Lines
### Endre Tót: *Very Special Gladness* (1971)

![Very Special Gladness](https://i.imgur.com/yWhgbqF.png)

*   **Medium**: Conceptual Art
*   **Mantra**: "I am glad if I can draw a line."
*   **Significance**: Uses mundane tasks as a strategy to navigate and subvert political and social constraints.
